**Rationale behind the ordering (brief):**
- Tier 1 covers the highest-frequency, pattern-defining problems (hashing, two-pointer basics, BFS/DFS on trees/grids, basic DP) that unlock nearly everything else.
- Tiers 2–3 build out the core patterns (binary search variants, heaps, backtracking, graph traversal, 1-D DP) that show up constantly in FAANG loops.
- Tier 4 rounds out "expected but slightly less universal" design/graph/interval problems.
- Tier 5–6 are mediums that are harder to derive from first principles or company-specific favorites (e.g., LRU-style design, bit tricks, top-K streaming).
- Tier 7–8 are genuinely hard problems (2-D DP, advanced graphs, hard string/array problems) that are high-value but lower-frequency — worth knowing once the fundamentals are locked in, but not where prep time should start.
